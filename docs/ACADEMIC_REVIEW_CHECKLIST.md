# Academic content review and approval checklist

## Status definitions
- DRAFT: authored and mapped; not approved for publication as authoritative exam preparation content.
- SUBJECT_REVIEW: awaiting review by a qualified Biology educator or relevant subject specialist.
- REVISION_REQUIRED: reviewer identified errors, ambiguity, weak explanations or syllabus gaps.
- APPROVED: all required checks passed and the reviewer, date and content version are recorded.
- RETIRED: superseded or withdrawn; retained only for audit history where appropriate.

## Lesson review
- [ ] Correctly mapped to the current JAMB and WAEC syllabus objectives.
- [ ] All scientific terms are defined before they are used in advanced explanations.
- [ ] Explanations are suitable for a learner with no assumed prior knowledge.
- [ ] Scientific statements, equations, examples and diagrams are accurate.
- [ ] Analogies are clearly limited and do not replace the scientific explanation.
- [ ] Nigerian examples are geographically and ecologically accurate.
- [ ] Worked examples show every calculation step and use correct units.
- [ ] Common misconceptions are identified and corrected.
- [ ] The knowledge check measures the stated learning objectives.
- [ ] The lesson distinguishes established facts from simplified teaching models.

## Objective-question review
- [ ] The item tests a named syllabus objective.
- [ ] The stem is clear, self-contained and free from avoidable ambiguity.
- [ ] Exactly one option is defensibly correct unless the question explicitly says otherwise.
- [ ] The answer key has been independently verified.
- [ ] Distractors are plausible and do not accidentally reveal the answer.
- [ ] The explanation teaches the underlying concept, not only the answer.
- [ ] Difficulty and cognitive demand are recorded.
- [ ] The item is original or has documented provenance and permission.
- [ ] It is not labelled as an official past question unless that provenance is verified.

## WAEC theory and practical review
- [ ] The task reflects the intended paper skill without claiming to reproduce an official paper.
- [ ] Marks add up and each marking point represents a defensible credit.
- [ ] Scientifically equivalent wording is accepted where appropriate.
- [ ] Practical procedures include controls, variables, observations and safety precautions.
- [ ] Model answers are clearly labelled as teaching examples, not official marking schemes.

## Reviewer record
Record the following before setting content to APPROVED:
- Content ID and version
- Syllabus version and objective mapping
- Reviewer name and relevant qualification
- Review date
- Decision: APPROVED or REVISION_REQUIRED
- Corrections made and rationale
- Source references and rights/licence status
- Next review date or syllabus-cycle trigger

## Release gate
Do not describe draft content as academically approved. Do not publish a generated question as an official past question. Keep the source, review decision and version available for audit.
