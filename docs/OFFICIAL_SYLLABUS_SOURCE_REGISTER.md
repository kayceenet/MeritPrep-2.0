# Official syllabus source register

**Last checked:** 25 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Keep syllabus mapping tied to primary examination-board sources. This register is a source record, not a claim that every statement has already been mapped or taught.

## JAMB UTME

The JAMB Integrated Brochure and Syllabus System (IBASS) is the official syllabus delivery route. The following official IBASS PDF copies were accessible during this review:

| Subject | Official source | Current repository mapping |
|---|---|---|
| Biology | https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/assets/uploads/Biology.pdf | `BiologyCurriculum`, `LearningCatalog` |
| Chemistry | https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/Chemistry.9efd0b98043d56922aaa.pdf | `ChemistryCourse` |
| Physics | https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/assets/uploads/Physics.pdf | `PhysicsCourse` and the five Physics course-extension files |

The Chemistry PDF was opened and its detailed syllabus headings and objectives were reviewed. It contains 18 numbered syllabus topics, from separation of mixtures through astronomical chemistry. The course currently has 18 corresponding topic entries. This is a topic-count comparison only; it does not establish statement-level completeness.

The Biology PDF begins with the detailed syllabus section on variety of organisms and specifies learning objectives for living organisms, cell structure, levels of organisation and organism diversity. Biology's current repository map has 24 learning units. These units still require an explicit statement-by-statement crosswalk to the current PDF.

The Physics PDF is the source used by the 39-topic ordered map. The 39 topic entries are currently assembled from the base Physics syllabus list and the five course-extension files. The exact objective statements and their placement in lessons still require a full crosswalk and subject review.

## WAEC WASSCE

Official WAEC Nigeria pages confirm that examination syllabuses are made available to candidates through the Council's website and list Biology, Chemistry and Physics as science subjects:

- WAEC FAQ / examination information: https://www.waecnigeria.org/index.php/node/9
- WASSCE entry regulations: https://www.waecnigeria.org/node/102

**Important limitation:** The exact current WAEC Biology, Chemistry and Physics syllabus documents have not yet been individually retrieved and version-checked in this register. The existing Biology curriculum's reference to WAEC Biology Section C is not, by itself, evidence of a complete WAEC crosswalk. Chemistry and Physics WAEC coverage must likewise be verified against the actual current documents before being marked audited.

## Required syllabus-audit workflow

1. Retrieve the current official PDF for each subject and examination (JAMB and WAEC separately).
2. Record its exact title, version/year, URL, access date and page count.
3. Extract every numbered topic, subtopic, content note and candidate objective.
4. Give every syllabus statement a stable internal identifier.
5. Map each statement to lesson sections, worked examples, diagrams/animations, simulations, virtual practicals and assessment items.
6. Record omissions, overlaps, differences between JAMB and WAEC, and any content beyond the stated examination scope.
7. Have a qualified subject reviewer verify the mapping and scientific accuracy.
8. Recheck the source each examination cycle; never infer that a prior year's PDF remains current.
