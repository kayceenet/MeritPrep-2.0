## Critical audit findings to preserve

- The official Biology syllabus includes many named examples and practical skills. A general paragraph on a topic is not enough; the listed organisms, structures, processes and applications must be taught and assessed.
- Ecology includes named Nigerian biomes, endemic diseases, sanitation, conservation organisations and Nigerian national parks/game reserves. These must not be omitted from a generic ecology overview.
- Variation includes actual measurement and frequency-distribution graph work, plus blood groups, fingerprints and physiological variation.
- Heredity includes meiosis/segregation, recombination, probability, sex determination, sex linkage and cross-breeding, not only a Punnett square.
- Biotechnology and evolution evidence are distinct examinable areas and must retain separate learning outcomes and assessments.
- A unit-to-lesson link test proves navigation coverage only. It does not prove that every official statement is taught, assessed, mastered or reviewed.

| Internal idea ID | JAMB syllabus scope represented | App evidence |
|---|---|---|

All six ideas have a dedicated check in Concepts, Examples and Context. Each check requires an initial response and a fresh transfer response. The linked practical uses explicit specimen clues and a sequential paired-choice key.
