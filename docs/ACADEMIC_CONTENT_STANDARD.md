# MeritPrep Academic Content Standard

## Status labels
- Draft: created but not reviewed; unavailable to learners by default.
- In review: awaiting subject-matter review.
- Approved: checked against a current official syllabus and suitable for publication.
- Retired: superseded or withdrawn; retained for audit history.

## Required provenance for every published item
- Exam board and examination track (JAMB UTME, WAEC WASSCE, or both)
- Syllabus edition/year and objective identifier
- Subject, topic and subtopic
- Source URL or bibliographic reference
- Copyright/licence and permitted use
- Author, subject reviewer and approval date
- Content version and change history

## Lesson quality checks
1. Correctly addresses a mapped syllabus objective.
2. Defines terms and explains mechanisms accurately.
3. Includes worked examples where relevant.
4. Uses accessible diagrams and meaningful alt descriptions.
5. Distinguishes simplifications from exact scientific statements.
6. Includes formative retrieval checks with explanations.
7. Has been proofread for language, units, notation and local context.

## Question quality checks
- Unambiguous stem and one defensible correct answer unless the format explicitly allows otherwise.
- Distractors are plausible and do not reveal the answer by grammar or length.
- Answer key and explanation independently verified.
- Source and rights recorded.
- Difficulty and objective tags assigned.
- Marking scheme versioned for constructed-response questions.

## Mastery policy
A lesson being opened or completed is not proof of mastery. Mastery should require repeated evidence across appropriate questions and a later retrieval opportunity. Report progress and confidence transparently; do not promise examination scores.
