# MeritPrep Physics curriculum and coverage map

**Exam track:** JAMB UTME  
**Source document:** JAMB IBASS Physics syllabus, served at https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/assets/uploads/Physics.pdf  
**Working map version:** `JAMB-IBASS-Physics-39-topic-map`  
**Content status:** draft; independent review by a qualified Physics teacher is outstanding.

## Source and scope

The topic headings below follow the 39 numbered topic headings in the Physics document served through JAMB's IBASS portal at the time this map was prepared. The official IBASS document should be checked again before each examination cycle. The map does not claim to be a WAEC Physics syllabus map, and it does not replace the detailed objectives and notes in the official document.

## Current implementation

The first thirty-two topics have beginner-focused lesson drafts, each with learning objectives, explanatory sections, a formative understanding check and a topic-linked original question set. The current bank contains 160 original objective questions, five per developed topic. The Physics practice screen shuffles the question order and answer options, records attempts locally and captures incorrect responses in the mistake notebook. The spaced-revision workflow also includes 97 Physics recall cards mapped across all thirty-two developed topics.

The remaining seven topics are syllabus outlines only. They cannot be marked as completed lessons. Physics content is draft material and has not received independent academic review.

### Virtual practical models

The Physics virtual-lab area includes interactive models for:
- Ohm's law, uniformly accelerated motion and perpendicular vector addition.
- Moments about a pivot and simple-machine mechanical advantage and efficiency.
- Hooke's law, spring force and elastic potential energy.
- Pressure from force and contact area.
- Density, hydrostatic gauge pressure and Archimedes' upthrust.
- Sensible heat transfer and latent heat during a phase change.
- Boyle's law and Charles's law, with absolute temperature in kelvin.
- Wave speed and period from frequency and wavelength.
- Echo sounding using round-trip sound travel time.
- Reflection at a plane mirror, with angles measured from the normal.

The calculation engines have unit tests for the main relationships and invalid input handling. These are idealised simulations: they do not collect sensor data, reproduce measurement uncertainty or replace a supervised practical investigation. Learners should still practise recording readings, plotting graphs, estimating uncertainty and evaluating sources of error.

## Interactive Physics models

The Physics tab in the virtual laboratory currently contains eight mathematical models:

- Ohm's law: adjustable voltage and resistance, with current and power outputs.
- Uniformly accelerated motion: initial velocity, acceleration and time, with final velocity and displacement.
- Perpendicular vector addition: two components, resultant magnitude and direction.
- Principle of moments: force and perpendicular arm lengths, with clockwise and anticlockwise moments.
- Simple-machine performance: load, effort, mechanical advantage, velocity ratio and efficiency, including a warning for impossible efficiency above 100%.
- Wave properties: frequency, wavelength, wave speed and period.
- Echo sounding: round-trip time and sound speed to estimate distance to a reflector.
- Plane-mirror reflection: equality of incident and reflected angles measured from the normal.

These are idealised calculation models, not physical laboratory instruments or complete practical examinations. Their assumptions and limitations are shown in the app. They do not yet assess experimental technique, measurement uncertainty or graph-drawing skill.

## Assessment integrity

- All 160 questions in the current Physics bank are original MeritPrep practice items.
- They must not be labelled as official JAMB or WAEC past questions.
- Every question has one keyed answer and an explanatory feedback note; the bank has structural unit tests for IDs, topic mapping, option count, answer-key bounds and provenance labels.
- The current 160-item practice set is not a full JAMB mock and is not a prediction of examination performance.
- The practice UI uses the shared local attempt and mistake-notebook workflow.
