# WAEC WASSCE Physics — provisional crosswalk

**Working reference:** [WAEC Physics 2026/2027](https://waecsyllabus.com/physics/)  
**Source status:** Secondary reproduction of WAEC syllabus text; direct primary-source PDF verification is pending.  
**Candidate scope:** Six syllabus sections, including the harmonised topics for short-structured questions. The reference page reports 42 topics.

## WAEC assessment requirements

The working reference describes:
- Paper 1: 50 multiple-choice questions, 1¼ hours, 50 marks.
- Paper 2: structured and essay questions, 1½ hours, 60 marks.
- Paper 3: practical for school candidates or alternative-to-practical for private candidates; three questions, answer two, 2¾ hours, 50 marks.

Candidates must be engaged in practical activities. All questions use SI units, though multiples/submultiples may appear.
