For each row, the release review must point to:
- lesson sections that teach each content statement from beginner level;
- worked examples and correct equations/calculations where applicable;
- diagrams, concept animations or simulations where they materially improve understanding;
- safe virtual practicals and physical-lab guidance where relevant;

- academic reviewer sign-off.

## Known crosswalk risks to resolve

- Topic 4 is much broader than an ionic formula builder: quantum numbers, orbital shapes/filling, periodic trends, coordinate bonding, intermolecular forces and molecular geometry all need explicit evidence.
- Topic 6 includes colloids, suspensions and solvent applications in addition to a solubility curve.
- Topic 8 includes pH/pOH, buffers, salt hydrolysis, conductance and titration curves, not titration alone.
- Topic 10 includes electrochemical cells, electrode potentials, corrosion and protection, not only charge passed.
- Topic 11 includes entropy and Gibbs free energy, not only calorimetry.
- Topic 14 spans several element families and compounds; a single qualitative-analysis activity is insufficient.
- Topic 16 covers many functional groups, reactions, tests, polymerisation and petroleum chemistry; a homologous-series formula builder is only one component.
- Topic 17 requires the range of industrial processes and their raw materials, not only Haber conditions.
- Topic 18 includes the solar system and Earth's spheres; spectral fingerprints alone do not cover the full topic.
